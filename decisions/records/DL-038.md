## Context

The built-in tempo stepper has a fixed range, 50–150% in steps of 10 (DL-016). A host with clean slow playback (DL-037) wants 20–200%, and sometimes knows the piece's tempo and would like to show it. A rate set through the API is not limited by the stepper: it is displayed as is, and 0.25 and 2 are tested in 0.3.0.

Browsers limit what an `<audio>` element can play: Firefox mutes media outside 0.25–4×, and older Safari caps the rate at 2. The pitch is kept only through the unprefixed `preservesPitch`, which iOS before 17.2 doesn't support.

## Decision

- **`tempo-min`, `tempo-max` and `tempo-step`**, in percent, with defaults 50, 150 and 10. A value that isn't a positive number falls back to its default. If `tempo-min` is above `tempo-max`, both fall back to their defaults.
- **The range bounds the built-in stepper only.** Its buttons move by `tempo-step` within the range, to a hundredth of a percent so that a fractional step stays on its grid, and disable at its limits. A rate set through the API is not limited by the range and is displayed as is, as in 0.3.0. Changing the attributes doesn't change the current rate, but the stepper's limits update at once, and its next step brings the rate into range.
- **The internal `<audio>`** is limited to 0.25–2, for the API and the stepper alike. The stepper's range is the attributes' range, each end held within 25–200%. An external media (DL-037) is not limited by the player and gets the attributes' full range.
- **`tempo-base`**: quarter notes per minute at 100%. When it is a positive number, the tempo control shows "♩ = N" beside the percentage (part `tempo-bpm`), where N is `tempo-base` × the rate, rounded, with an accessible text in the player's language ("N quarter notes per minute", "N fjärdedelar per minut").
- The stepper stays native buttons, usable from the keyboard with the controls' focus ring. There are no tempo shortcuts, so none need to follow the range.
- The internal `<audio>` sets `webkitPreservesPitch` beside `preservesPitch`.

## Rationale

- Percent attributes match what the stepper shows, and their defaults keep 0.3.0's behaviour for every existing page.
- Bounding only the stepper keeps the API backwards compatible: a site that sets a rate itself has chosen it.
- A rate the internal `<audio>` can't play would mute or fail rather than be slow, so the limit protects listeners in every browser. An external media knows its own limits.
- Showing the tempo in quarter notes helps musicians who think in metronome marks. It is optional because only the host knows the piece's tempo.

## Consequences

- With the internal `<audio>`, setting a rate outside 0.25–2 through the API is clamped. In 0.3.0 such a rate was passed to the browser and could mute or throw.
- `tempo-base` assumes a quarter-note beat. A piece in another beat unit shows its tempo in quarter notes.
