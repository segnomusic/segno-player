## Context

After trying the demo locally, the maintainer prefers a simpler header without the engraved final-barline rule recorded in DL-021.

## Decision

Remove the decorative double rule above the demo header by deleting `.hero-header::before`. Keep the header bottom divider and the existing logo, typography, colors, score borders and other layout rules unchanged.

## Rationale

The simpler header better matches the preferred visual balance without changing the rest of the Segno identity.
