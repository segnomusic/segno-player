## Context

Full engraved SVG pages become difficult to read on narrow screens. The canvas-viewport spike demonstrated that a zoomed two-system view and a two-row bar-group view can make playback usable on phones while retaining the source engraving. Full pages remain the clean default and the reference rendering.

## Decision

`<segno-player>` supports full-score, two-system, and reflow score views. Two-system crops the original pages around engraved system pairs. Reflow crops and recombines whole bar regions into new rows at a locked scale. Neither view moves individual symbols, interprets notation, re-engraves music, or alters the source SVG.

Full score remains the existing page-image renderer and the initial default. The alternative views are rendering options of the same player, not a separate notation engine.

## Rationale

This extends the original purpose of playing unaltered, manually laid-out engravings rather than replacing it. Cropping and recomposition change which engraved regions are visible and where whole regions sit, but preserve every glyph position within those regions. Keeping full score as the default preserves the established contract and makes the mobile adaptations an explicit choice.

## Consequences

- DL-014 gains alternative canvas views alongside the original full-page images and overlays.
- The no-reflow consequence of DL-001 is narrowed to notation re-engraving and symbol-level layout, which remain out of scope.
- Bar and system crops must retain enough context and padding to avoid clipping notation.
- Multi-page scores, repeat-aware playback, seeking, and click-to-play must work in every view.
