## Context

Zoom and reflow currently derive their shared scale from the widest two-bar group. Engravings with wider bars therefore show smaller notation even when their staff size is identical. Real Fria Noter exports also vary in whether `.mpos` bar height covers a staff or a whole system, so that height cannot calibrate optical size.

## Decision

Measure staff-line spacing within each `.mpos` system's vertical bounds, require a corresponding SVG `BarLine` extent, reject measurements inconsistent with those bounds, and use the median resulting staff height to choose one score-wide content width of 15 staff heights, calibrated against the readable Härlig är jorden rendering. This excludes gaps between one-line staves and between systems, conservatively falling back when the geometry is ambiguous. Use that width for both two-system crops and reflow rows so equal engraved staff sizes have equal CSS size at the same player width.

Form groups of up to two consecutive bars within a system, but start a new group when adding another bar would exceed the selected content width. Expand the width only when an individual padded bar cannot fit. Keep the existing group-window, horizontal crop, tile-padding, page-size and playback behavior. When no usable staff lines are present, retain the previous widest-two-bar-group calculation as a fallback.

Measure the bottom of MuseScore SVG `Lyrics` geometry as well. Associate lyrics with the preceding system on the same page and extend that system's content bottom to include them in both two-system crops and reflow tiles. This replaces reliance on `.mpos` heights, which cover the whole system in some exports but only the staff in others. Without usable lyric geometry, retain the previous vertical bounds.

## Rationale

Staff-line spacing directly represents the engraved scale, unlike bar widths or variably reported system bounds. Width-aware grouping prevents an unusually wide pair from reducing the scale of every other group while retaining the original engraving and a common isotropic scale. Measuring lyric geometry supplies the content extent that short `.mpos` system bounds omit, without changing notation or symbol positions.

## Consequences

- DL-022 and DL-023 gain staff-relative scale calibration and width-aware grouping; symbol-level layout and notation interpretation remain out of scope.
- Layout tests cover staff and lyric measurement, equal scaling across different bar widths, oversized groups, lyric-aware crop/tile extents, page sizes and the no-staff fallback.
- Six real Fria Noter exports provide before/after browser validation, including second-system lyrics in both views, before any push.
