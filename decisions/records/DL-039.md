## Context

DL-017 (and DL-005's safety checks) turn the markers off when the audio ends before the last event starts. That check exists for one export mistake: with "Play repeats" off, the audio lacks whole passes while the position files always unroll repeats, so every marker after the first repeat would be wrong. The audio is then shorter by at least one pass, usually many bars.

The same check also fires on a sound score. The last event is the start of the last bar, and audio can end before that bar starts when the bar is silent: a final bar of rests, or a closing fermata over a rest. MuseScore's MP3 usually runs past it with its release tail, but audio that stops at the last sound does not, for example a synthesizer's playback given through `media` (DL-037), or trimmed audio. The player then dropped every marker for the whole piece, though everything before the last bar was in step.

## Decision

**The audio is too short only when it ends before the second-to-last event starts.** A shortfall smaller than that, where only the last event starts after the audio's end, is tolerated: the markers stay on and nothing is logged. A sync map with one event keeps the old rule (too short when the audio ends before that event), as there is no second-to-last event to measure from. The check reads the current media's duration and runs again on `durationchange` and on each swap of `media`, as before, so it applies to the internal `<audio>` and to a host's media alike.

**At the end of the media, the markers show the last event.** While the media's `ended` is true, or its `currentTime` has reached its `duration`, the player places the markers as if playback were at the last event's time. The listener sees the piece reach its last bar, and `currentBar` and `barchange` report it. Where the audio runs past the last event, this changes nothing.

**`playBar()` on a bar past the end.** When the pass `playBar()` picks starts after the media's duration, the player seeks to the end, which marks the bar, and resolves without calling `play()`. Calling `play()` there would restart a media element from the beginning.

The real mismatch keeps its console warning and its "Play repeats" hint. The sync map format and the drawing of the markers don't change, and the player doesn't look at the audio's content to guess silent bars.

## Rationale

A threshold at the second-to-last event scales with the score's tempo and bar lengths, where a fixed number of seconds would be too strict for slow music and too lax for fast music. Ending before the last bar's start is common in sound scores, and dropping every marker for it is worse than a last bar marked at the very end.

The cost is detection: a missing repeat pass is now caught only when it is longer than the second-to-last bar, which before was not needed. A missing pass of a single short bar was already easily hidden by an audio's tail, so the check was never exact; it is meant for the usual case, where whole sections are missing.

## Consequences

- With a silent last bar, the last bar's marker appears when the media ends, not when the bar would start, and the bar's progress isn't shown.
- A site that seeks past the media's end through `currentTime` gets the media's own behaviour; only `playBar()` clamps.
