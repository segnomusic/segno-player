`<segno-player>` (`@segnomusic/player/player`): plays a score's audio on its SVG pages with a bar marker and a note cursor. Its playback API follows `HTMLMediaElement`, it has optional built-in controls, and it can be themed with `--segno-*` custom properties, parts and container queries. When the files don't fit together it plays without markers and says why.
